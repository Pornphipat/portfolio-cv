2026 – now

Muny: AI research assistant for antibiotic drug design

MANORAA, Mahidol University. AI lead in a team of 3.

Answers questions about 406 protein–ligand structures (51 antibiotics), and refuses when the data has no answer.

  • Hybrid retrieval: vector search plus exact lexical match, so PDB IDs and ligand codes hit the right record.
  • An anti-hallucination guard that refuses instead of inventing evidence.
  • A FastAPI service with API-key and session-token auth, rate limiting and automated API tests, deployed with Docker on the university server.

Hardest problem: exact-ID questions kept returning the wrong structure, because an ID like 1ABC means nothing to an embedding model. I drop distant vector hits so those queries fall through to exact lexical search, and keep on-screen UI text out of the search query.
